Dirty Money - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Dirty Money is a single-player mobile game published by Dirty Money Games. We respect player privacy.

Information We Collect

Dirty Money does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, or other personal information to play.

The game stores saved progress, settings, and high scores locally on the player's device. This information is used to support gameplay and is not transmitted to Dirty Money Games by the game.

Advertising and Analytics

Dirty Money does not currently include advertising or third-party analytics in its game code.

Sharing of Information

Dirty Money Games does not sell or share player information collected through the game with third parties.

Data Storage and Deletion

Saved game information is stored locally on the device. Players may be able to remove this information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app, subject to their device's backup and restore settings.

Children's Privacy

Dirty Money contains mature crime-related themes and is not designed for children. Players should follow the age rating shown on the app's store listing.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy if the game's features or data practices change. Any updated policy will be published on this page.

Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact Dirty Money Games at dirtymoneyau@outlook.com.
